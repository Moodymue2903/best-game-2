## Concepts proposed
1. **TIDEBREAKER** — you are the moon; drag the moon to raise/lower tides on a water-column grid, shepherd a harvester boat to port, monsters surface in shallow water. 8 nights; second moon night 5; pinned moon night 7; persistent "tide scars". Risk: gravity mechanic needs a paragraph to explain; abstract on a contact sheet; risks reading as "another water game" vs Lumen Reef / Lamplight Tide.
2. **HOLLOWS** — you are the house; rotate/slide rooms (Rubik's-cube rows/columns) to line up matching moods into corridors that settle and produce resources; Hollow Men walk your corridors toward the heart-room. 10 nights, 3x3 growing to spiral; 12 relics between nights. Visual: candle-amber dollhouse cross-section vs cold blue-black exterior; black silhouettes with white pinprick eyes. Risk: busy floor plan needs a second look; risks "another defend-the-heart game".
3. **ASHFALL GARDEN** — you are the gardener of a dying volcano; drag to carve channels in deformable ash terrain, steer lava away from crops / toward enemies / toward a cold spring to make obsidian. Lava has heat + momentum; channels too steep overshoot, too shallow clog. Crops grow only in a narrow temperature band. 10 YEARS (seasons, breaks the night structure): new crop each year (ember peppers, glass melons, frost-root, ash roses), new vent, longer eruptions; cold spring year 4 (obsidian walls); lava elementals year 7 collapse channels; year 10 final eruption — one perfect channel carrying the whole flow past the garden into the sea. Between years: pick 1 of 3 seed-mutations. Win a year: harvest quota before eruption ends. Lose a year: garden burns or quota missed. Win game: survive 10 years + final channel. Lose game: garden burns twice. Visual: charcoal-black ash, thick orange lava ribbons with pulsing glow, jewel-bright crop dots (green/pink/cyan). Audio: deep rumble, cooling hiss, kalimba pluck on ripen.

## DeepSeek's PICK: ASHFALL GARDEN
Reasons: (1) Photographability — instant in one frame (black/orange/jewel), most legible of the three; TIDEBREAKER reads as "lines", HOLLOWS needs a second look. (2) Mechanical legibility — verb understood in one sentence/gesture; failure state visible. (3) Progression — 10 years with new crop/vent/mutation each year is the cleanest judge-friendly arc; final eruption is a memorable ending. (4) Least clone-like — no farming/volcano/terraforming in the top 10; furthest from every winner. (5) Degrades gracefully — lava flow can fall back to simplest lowest-path rule and the game still works.
