## Concepts proposed (all scoped 0.8-1.5 MB, vanilla JS + canvas + WebAudio, ~24h, explicit WIN/LOSE on screen, dense HUD, title screen, 7-stage progression, one clear verb)
1. **ASHLAND** — verb SHAPE SMOKE. Tend a dying fire: hold to inhale smoke, drag to exude it as a solid shape (bridge, wall, ramp, projectile, sail). Smoke solidifies on release, holds ~4s, disperses. Every shape costs fire: fire = health bar AND resource bar. 7 nights, each adds a rule: Bridges (cross gaps to kindling), Walls (block cold wind), Projectiles (knock fruit to feed fire), Ramps (redirect rolling embers), Cover (hide fire from Frost Wraith searchlights), Sails (push a spark across a frozen lake), Sandcastle (combine all five to last till dawn). Visual: charcoal #1a1410, ember #ff7a2a, smoke #c8b8a0, cold #6b8aa8, cream text #f4e8d0; cold blue fog creeps from edges (loss made visible); bloom on fire, drifting sparks, vignette tightening as fuel drops. Win: fire lit at dawn (7 nights). Lose: fire dies. Why it wins: sculpting smoke as transient solid is not in the top 10; fire tradeoff = depth from one mechanic; screenshots read instantly. Risk: shape recognition (line/ramp/blob/projectile classification of freehand strokes) is the riskiest core to tune — a misread bridge breaks playability.
2. **DEEPCALL** — verb PING. World is pure black; SPACE emits a ping, returning sound briefly reveals geometry as cyan contours fading over ~2s. Soft ping (small reveal, no predators) vs loud ping (wide reveal, predators home in). 7 depths: navigate to vent, collect pearls, Pale Fish hunts loud pings, piggyback on enemy pings, thermal currents carry pings, resonant crystals need specific pitch, escape the Leviathan (it pings back). Visual: pure #000208, cyan #4dd0e1 contours, magenta/teal bioluminescence; heavy reverb, whale-song drones, sub-bass. Win: escape depth 7. Lose: caught or breath empties. Why: most original verb, inverts the light theme. Risk: screenshots read as "empty black screen" unless perfectly composed — highest variance.
3. **HEARTHBORN** — verbs GIVE HEAT / TAKE HEAT. Carry one coal: left-click gives heat (melt/ignite/warm), right-click takes heat (freeze/harden/cool); heat transfers through materials at different rates. 7 frozen realms, each rescues a frozen traveler: melt ice, chain heat through metal bars, freeze water into bridges, ignite gunpowder, cool lava into obsidian platforms, thermal valves, final Frozen King (warm his heart + freeze his crown simultaneously). Visual: thermal-imaging palette, hot #ff5722-#ffb74d, cold #1a237e-#311b92, neutral cream #f5e6d3. Win: thaw 7 travelers. Lose: coal goes cold or a traveler freezes. Why: dual verb rarer than single-verb; thermal palette photographs well. Risk: reads "thinky/slow" vs action-heavy top 10; needs 90-second solves.

## GLM's PICK: ASHLAND
Reasons: (1) Screenshot legibility — orange smoke shapes on black read in under a second; DEEPCALL risks "mostly empty black", HEARTHBORN may not parse at a glance. (2) Risk-adjusted scope — particle field + geometric shape detection is most tractable in 24h. (3) Tightest core loop — "use smoke = lose fire" creates tension/resource/emergence without an upgrade shop. DEEPCALL = higher ceiling, higher variance. HEARTHBORN = deeper puzzle craft, slower pace. ASHLAND = best expected value.
